# Requirements Document

## Introduction

One workspace named "Policy Follow-up" containing exactly two tabs: **Renewals** and **Cancellations**.

Phase 1 revises the existing Renewals user interface without changing its working business logic, imports, database functions, or outcomes. Phase 2 adds a new Cancellations module that imports cancellation reports, sends documented SMS and email reminders on a fixed schedule, and tracks payment, reinstatement, and cancellation outcomes.

### Current-state audit (verified in this repository)

- Renewals feature code: `src/features/renewals/api.ts`, `RenewalsPage.tsx`, `PowerBiRenewalImport.tsx`. Route: `src/app/tools/renewals/page.tsx`, gated by `canAccessRenewals`.
- Renewals data: `renewal_records`, `renewal_contacts`, `renewal_events`, `renewal_import_runs`, `renewal_assignment_aliases`, `renewal_sms_log`; RPCs `renewal_import_batch`, `renewal_update_workflow`, `renewal_manager_update`, `renewal_assign`, `renewal_send_to_requote`, `renewal_upsert_assignment_alias`, `renewal_delete_assignment_alias`, `renewal_update_contact_info`.
- SMS provider exists: `src/lib/ringcentral-sms.ts` (`sendSms`, `sendBatchSms`, `getMessageStatus`, `getBatchStatus`), credentials read server-side from `RC_*` environment variables.
- Scheduler pattern exists: `POST /api/renewals/sms/scheduler`, authorized by `Bearer ${CRON_SECRET}` or by a `manager` / `super_admin` session, using a service-role Supabase client.
- Internal notifications exist: `public.user_notifications` (+ `src/features/notifications`). Its `notification_type` check constraint currently allows only `'turn'` and `'assignment'`.
- Evidence upload pattern exists: Supabase Storage bucket `renewal-contact-evidence` with signed-URL download.
- Module registry: `src/platform/module-registry.ts` consumed by `src/features/platform/ToolsHub.tsx`.
- **No email provider exists** in this repository (no Resend, SendGrid, Nodemailer, or SMTP dependency or helper). Email delivery is genuinely new infrastructure.
- **No canonical `customers` or `policies` table exists.** Customer and policy matching must resolve against existing renewal/cancellation data keys (`hawksoft_client_id` equivalent, policy number), not against a shared customer master table.
- Test tooling: `vitest` (`npm test` runs `vitest --run`) and `fast-check` are already installed.

## Glossary

- **Policy_Follow_Up_Workspace**: The single workspace containing the Renewals tab and the Cancellations tab.
- **Renewals_Module**: The existing renewal tracking feature, revised in Phase 1.
- **Cancellations_Module**: The new cancellation tracking feature added in Phase 2.
- **Renewal_Record**: One row of `renewal_records`, keyed by policy number and renewal date.
- **Cancellation_Case**: One cancellation event, uniquely keyed by policy number plus cancellation effective date.
- **Cancellation_Importer**: The component that parses, maps, validates, and loads cancellation report rows.
- **Contact_Recipient**: One individual phone number or one individual email address belonging to a Cancellation_Case, stored as its own row.
- **Touchpoint**: One of the four scheduled reminder milestones: 15, 10, 5, or 1 day before the cancellation effective date.
- **Notification_Scheduler**: The server process that selects due Touchpoints and requests sends.
- **Message_Renderer**: The component that produces a subject and body from a stored template version and Cancellation_Case data.
- **Communication_Record**: One stored row describing a single rendered message sent to one Contact_Recipient over one channel for one Touchpoint.
- **Idempotency_Key**: The tuple (Cancellation_Case, Contact_Recipient, Touchpoint, channel) that uniquely identifies a Communication_Record.
- **Case_Status**: The state of the cancellation situation: Imported, Open, Payment Reported, Verification Pending, Reinstatement Pending, Reinstated, Cancelled, Resolved, Invalid, Duplicate.
- **Communication_Status**: The state of outbound messaging for a Cancellation_Case: Not Scheduled, Scheduled, Partially Sent, Sent, Delivered, Partially Failed, Failed, Suppressed, Manual Follow-up Required.
- **Manager_Role**: A profile whose role is `manager` or `super_admin`.
- **Agent_Role**: A profile whose role is `agent`, `customer_service`, or `sales_supervisor`, that is, a profile granted Policy_Follow_Up_Workspace access without Manager_Role privileges.
- **Agency_Name**: The literal string "New Hope Insurance Agency".
- **Office_Phone**: The agency office telephone number rendered in every reminder.
- **Current_Business_Date**: The calendar date in the agency local time zone at the moment a date comparison is evaluated. Every renewal date, cancellation effective date, follow-up date, and Touchpoint date is compared to it as a whole calendar date, disregarding time of day.
- **Prohibited_Phrase_List**: The stored list of English and Spanish phrases that no rendered subject and no rendered body may contain, covering claims of reinstatement, claims that payment guarantees coverage, requests for payment-card or bank account data, and claims of being the carrier's official legal notice.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Policy Follow-up Workspace Shell

**User Story:** As an employee, I want one workspace with a Renewals tab and a Cancellations tab, so that I can work both policy follow-up queues without leaving the page.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Policy_Follow_Up_Workspace SHALL present exactly two tabs, labeled Renewals and Cancellations, ordered Renewals first and Cancellations second.
2. WHEN a user loads the Policy_Follow_Up_Workspace page, THE Policy_Follow_Up_Workspace SHALL select the Renewals tab, independent of the tab that was selected during any earlier page load.
3. WHEN a user switches tabs, THE Policy_Follow_Up_Workspace SHALL preserve the search text, saved filter selection, and sort order of the tab being left for the remainder of the current page load, SHALL restore those three values when the user returns to that tab, and SHALL close the detail drawer of the tab being left.
4. THE Policy_Follow_Up_Workspace SHALL render each tab with a summary filter bar above the list surface, a search field, a saved filter control, a list surface, a detail drawer anchored to the right edge of the viewport, an activity timeline inside the detail drawer ordered by event time with the most recent event first, and exactly one visually prominent primary action control for the selected record.
5. WHILE a tab is loading its first page of at most 50 records, THE Policy_Follow_Up_Workspace SHALL display a loading indicator in place of the list surface until the query returns records or returns an error.
6. WHILE a saved filter is selected or search text is present, WHEN a tab query returns zero records, THE Policy_Follow_Up_Workspace SHALL display an empty state that names the selected saved filter and the search text and offers a control that clears both the saved filter selection and the search text.
7. IF a tab query returns an error or does not return within 15 seconds, THEN THE Policy_Follow_Up_Workspace SHALL display a message indicating that the record query failed together with a retry control, SHALL retain the records loaded for that tab before the failed query, and SHALL retain the search text, saved filter selection, and sort order unchanged.
8. THE Policy_Follow_Up_Workspace SHALL expose no control that creates a to-do item, a task, a task template, a task board, a drag-and-drop task arrangement, or a user-defined workflow state.
9. WHILE no saved filter is selected and no search text is present, WHEN a tab query returns zero records, THE Policy_Follow_Up_Workspace SHALL display an empty state indicating that the tab contains zero records and SHALL omit the clearing control described in criterion 6.
10. WHEN a user selects the retry control, THE Policy_Follow_Up_Workspace SHALL re-run the record query for the active tab using the retained search text, saved filter selection, and sort order, and SHALL display a loading indicator until the re-run returns records or returns an error.
11. WHEN a user switches tabs, THE Policy_Follow_Up_Workspace SHALL render the target tab's list surface or loading indicator within 1 second and without reloading the page.

1. 2. THE Renewals_Module SHALL offer exactly the renewal outcome values Renewed, Lost, and Cancelled, and SHALL reject a submitted outcome value outside that set.
3. THE Renewals_Module SHALL offer exactly the contact channel values call, SMS, WhatsApp, email, in person, and other, and SHALL reject a contact entry whose channel value is outside that set.
4. WHERE a capability named in this criterion is performable through the functions listed in criterion 1, THE Renewals_Module SHALL keep that capability invocable from the Renewals tab of the Policy_Follow_Up_Workspace and SHALL store, for identical user input, the same field values the pre-revision implementation stored, for each of these capabilities: renewal import, assignment mapping, employee assignment, renewal date, current premium, renewal premium, customer phone, customer email, contact logging, required notes, evidence upload, next follow-up date, requote creation, renewal history, and manager editing.
5. WHEN a user requests the Renewals tab, THE Renewals_Module SHALL authorize the request in server-side code by evaluating `canAccessRenewals` against the signed-in profile role, with the set of roles `canAccessRenewals` accepts unchanged from the pre-revision set.
6. WHEN a defect is found in existing renewal business logic, THE Renewals_Module SHALL record the defect in the spec task list with the affected database function or feature file, the observed behavior, and the expected behavior, and SHALL leave the functions listed in criterion 1 unchanged until a profile holding Manager_Role approves the recorded task.
7. IF a call to a renewal database function returns an error, THEN THE Renewals_Module SHALL display an error message naming the attempted renewal operation, SHALL retain the values already entered in the open form, and SHALL leave the affected Renewal_Record and its contact entries unchanged.
8. THE Renewals_Module SHALL exclude changes to the stored definitions of the functions listed in criterion 1 and exclude removals and renames of existing columns of `renewal_records`, `renewal_contacts`, `renewal_events`, `renewal_import_runs`, `renewal_assignment_aliases`, and `renewal_sms_log`, and SHALL retain every row those tables hold before the revision is deployed.
9. IF `canAccessRenewals` returns false for the signed-in profile, THEN THE Renewals_Module SHALL deny the request, SHALL return zero Renewal_Record rows, and SHALL leave every row of the tables listed in criterion 8 unchanged.
10. IF a capability named in criterion 4 is performable only through a direct table write or through a replacement function because the functions listed in criterion 1 do not perform it, THEN THE Renewals_Module SHALL treat criterion 1 as prevailing over criterion 4, SHALL record that capability as a defect under criterion 6, SHALL exclude every direct table write and every replacement function for that capability, and SHALL leave that capability outside the revision until a profile holding Manager_Role approves the recorded task.

### Requirement 3: Renewals Summary Filters

**User Story:** As an agent, I want clickable summary counters, so that one click narrows the renewal list to the work I need to do next.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Renewals_Module SHALL present exactly these summary filters: Overdue follow-up, Follow-up due today, Due within 3 days, Due within 7 days, Due within 15 days, Due within 30 days, No contact recorded, Waiting on customer, Requote requested, and Customer decision pending.
2. WHEN a user selects a summary filter, THE Renewals_Module SHALL restrict the renewal list to exactly the Renewal_Record rows counted by that filter, SHALL keep at most one summary filter active, and SHALL keep the active search text applied.
3. THE Renewals_Module SHALL display, for each of the ten summary filters, a count of the Renewal_Record rows that are within the read scope of the signed-in profile AND match the active search text, SHALL compute every count independently of which summary filter is active, and SHALL count one Renewal_Record in every summary filter whose counting rule that record satisfies.
4. WHEN a user selects the currently active summary filter, THE Renewals_Module SHALL clear that filter, SHALL restore the renewal list to every Renewal_Record within the read scope of the signed-in profile that matches the active search text, and SHALL leave the active search text unchanged.
5. THE Renewals_Module SHALL treat a Renewal_Record as being in an open status WHERE no renewal outcome of Renewed, Lost, or Cancelled has been recorded for that record, and SHALL exclude every Renewal_Record that is not in an open status from every summary filter count and from every summary filter result set.
6. THE Renewals_Module SHALL evaluate every summary filter against the current business date, defined as the calendar date in the agency local time zone at the moment the filter is evaluated, and SHALL compare every next follow-up date and every renewal date to the current business date as whole calendar dates, disregarding time of day.
7. WHERE an open Renewal_Record has a next follow-up date earlier than the current business date, THE Renewals_Module SHALL count that record in Overdue follow-up, WHERE an open Renewal_Record has a next follow-up date equal to the current business date, THE Renewals_Module SHALL count that record in Follow-up due today, and WHERE an open Renewal_Record has no next follow-up date, THE Renewals_Module SHALL exclude that record from both Overdue follow-up and Follow-up due today and SHALL keep that record eligible for the remaining summary filters.
8. WHERE an open Renewal_Record has a renewal date on or after the current business date AND on or before the current business date plus N calendar days, THE Renewals_Module SHALL count that record in Due within N days for each of N equal to 3, 7, 15, and 30, SHALL treat both window bounds as inclusive, SHALL treat the four windows as cumulative so that a record counted in a shorter window is also counted in every longer window, and SHALL exclude from all four windows every Renewal_Record whose renewal date is absent or earlier than the current business date.
9. WHERE an open Renewal_Record has zero contact entries, THE Renewals_Module SHALL count that record in No contact recorded, and WHERE an open Renewal_Record has a current status of Waiting on customer, Requote requested, or Customer decision pending, THE Renewals_Module SHALL count that record in the summary filter carrying the same name and in no other status-based summary filter.

### Requirement 4: Renewals List Surface

**User Story:** As an agent, I want one compact row per renewal with the decision fields visible, so that I can triage without opening each record.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Renewals_Module SHALL display, in each renewal row, exactly these values: customer name, policy number, carrier, line of business, renewal date, days remaining, assigned employee, current premium, renewal premium, premium change, last contact, next follow-up, status, and next required action, where last contact is the event date of the most recent contact entry recorded on the Renewal_Record, status is the renewal workflow status stored on the Renewal_Record, and next required action is the recommended next action value defined in Requirement 5 criterion 2.
2. THE Renewals_Module SHALL order the complete filtered renewal result set, before pagination, by applying these sort keys in this sequence: first, Renewal_Record rows counted in Overdue follow-up as defined in Requirement 3 criterion 7 before all other rows; second, renewal date ascending; third, Renewal_Record rows counted in No contact recorded as defined in Requirement 3 criterion 9 before all other remaining rows; fourth, customer name ascending compared without case sensitivity; fifth, policy number ascending compared character by character without case sensitivity.
3. WHERE a Renewal_Record has no assigned employee, THE Renewals_Module SHALL display "Unassigned" in the assigned employee cell.
4. WHERE either the current premium or the renewal premium of a Renewal_Record is absent, THE Renewals_Module SHALL display an em dash in the premium change cell.
5. WHEN a user enters search text, THE Renewals_Module SHALL restrict the renewal list, within the currently active summary filter and no later than 1 second after the most recently entered character, to Renewal_Record rows whose customer name, policy number, carrier, customer phone, or customer email contains the effective search text as a substring matched without case sensitivity, where the effective search text is the entered text with leading and trailing whitespace removed and limited to its first 100 characters, and where an effective search text of zero characters matches every Renewal_Record.
6. WHEN a user selects a renewal row, THE Renewals_Module SHALL open the renewal detail drawer for that Renewal_Record while retaining the active summary filter, the search text, and the sort order of the renewal list.
7. THE Renewals_Module SHALL display, in the days remaining cell, the count of whole calendar days from the current business date used by the Requirement 3 summary filters to the renewal date of the Renewal_Record, expressed as a whole number that equals 0 when the renewal date is the current business date and is less than 0 when the renewal date precedes the current business date.
8. WHERE both the current premium and the renewal premium of a Renewal_Record are present, THE Renewals_Module SHALL display, in the premium change cell, the renewal premium minus the current premium rounded to two decimal places, carrying a leading minus sign when that result is less than zero, a leading plus sign when that result is greater than zero, and no sign when that result equals zero.
9. WHERE a row value listed in criterion 1 other than assigned employee and premium change is absent for a Renewal_Record, THE Renewals_Module SHALL display an em dash in that cell.

### Requirement 5: Renewal Detail Drawer

**User Story:** As an agent, I want one drawer that holds every renewal action, so that I complete the whole follow-up in one place.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHILE the renewal detail drawer is open for a Renewal_Record, THE Renewals_Module SHALL display the customer name, policy number, carrier, line of business, renewal date, days remaining, current premium, renewal premium, premium change amount, premium change percentage, customer phone, customer email, assigned employee, current status, the recommended next action, the contact composer, the notes and evidence of each contact entry, the next follow-up date, the requote activity, the chronological timeline, and the final outcome actions, SHALL display "Unassigned" WHERE the Renewal_Record has no assigned employee, and SHALL display an em dash in place of every other displayed value that is absent.
2. WHILE the renewal detail drawer is open for a Renewal_Record in an open status, THE Renewals_Module SHALL display exactly one recommended next action, drawn from exactly the set Make first contact, Complete follow-up, Prepare requote, Review requote, Record customer decision, and Close renewal, selected as the action of the first rule below whose condition holds, evaluated in this fixed order:
   - Rule 1, Close renewal: a final outcome value of Renewed, Lost, or Cancelled is recorded for the Renewal_Record.
   - Rule 2, Record customer decision: the current status of the Renewal_Record is Customer decision pending.
   - Rule 3, Review requote: the Renewal_Record has at least one requote activity entry and has zero contact entries whose contact time is later than the most recent requote activity time.
   - Rule 4, Prepare requote: the current status of the Renewal_Record is Requote requested and the Renewal_Record has zero requote activity entries.
   - Rule 5, Make first contact: the Renewal_Record has zero contact entries.
   - Rule 6, Complete follow-up: no condition of Rules 1 through 5 holds.
3. WHILE the renewal detail drawer displays a recommended next action, THE Renewals_Module SHALL render the action control that performs that recommended next action as the only visually prominent primary action control in the renewal detail drawer, and SHALL render every other action control in the renewal detail drawer as a non-prominent control.
4. IF a user submits a contact entry whose notes text contains zero non-whitespace characters or whose notes text exceeds 5,000 characters, THEN THE Renewals_Module SHALL reject the submission, SHALL display a message identifying the notes requirement that failed, SHALL retain the entered channel, notes text, and attached evidence in the contact composer, and SHALL leave the chronological timeline unchanged.
5. WHEN a user submits a contact entry whose notes text contains at least one non-whitespace character and at most 5,000 characters and whose channel is one of call, SMS, WhatsApp, email, in person, or other, THE Renewals_Module SHALL store the contact entry with its contact time, recording profile, selected channel, notes text, and every attached evidence reference, and SHALL display the stored entry as the most recent chronological timeline entry without requiring the drawer to be closed and reopened.
6. IF a user attaches an evidence file whose size exceeds 100 megabytes, or attaches more than 10 evidence files to one contact entry, THEN THE Renewals_Module SHALL reject that attachment, SHALL display the limit that was exceeded, and SHALL retain every previously accepted attachment and the entered channel and notes text in the contact composer.
7. IF an evidence upload returns a failure or does not complete within 120 seconds, THEN THE Renewals_Module SHALL leave the contact entry unsaved, SHALL display the upload failure reason, SHALL retain the entered channel and notes text in the contact composer, SHALL offer a retry control, and SHALL leave the chronological timeline unchanged.
8. THE Renewals_Module SHALL include in the chronological timeline every contact entry, every status change, every assignment change, every next follow-up date change, every requote activity entry, and every recorded final outcome of the Renewal_Record, ordered by event time with the most recent event first, and SHALL order two entries with equal event times by placing the later-recorded entry first.
9. IF a user submits a contact entry whose channel is absent or is not one of call, SMS, WhatsApp, email, in person, or other, THEN THE Renewals_Module SHALL reject the submission, SHALL display a message that a channel selection from the permitted channels is required, SHALL retain the entered notes text and attached evidence in the contact composer, and SHALL leave the chronological timeline unchanged.
10. IF a user submits a next follow-up date that is earlier than the current business date or later than 365 days after the current business date, THEN THE Renewals_Module SHALL reject the submission, SHALL display the permitted date range, and SHALL retain the previously stored next follow-up date.
11. WHEN a user selects a final outcome action, THE Renewals_Module SHALL require exactly one outcome value from Renewed, Lost, and Cancelled and note text containing at least one non-whitespace character before storing the outcome, and SHALL store the outcome with the recording profile and the outcome time.

### Requirement 8: Cancellation Report Import

**User Story:** As a manager, I want to upload a cancellation report and review it before it loads, so that bad data never reaches customers.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHERE the signed-in profile holds Manager_Role, THE Cancellation_Importer SHALL accept an uploaded CSV file whose first row is a header row, whose size is no larger than 25 megabytes, and which contains 1 to 20,000 data rows.
2. WHEN the header row of an uploaded file contains both `Poliza` and `FechaCancelacion`, matched after removing leading and trailing whitespace and matched without case sensitivity, THE Cancellation_Importer SHALL classify the file as the `eficacia` column set, SHALL accept the columns `Cliente`, `Poliza`, `Compania`, `FechaCancelacion`, `MontoDebido`, `Enviar`, `Estado`, `Resultado`, `AvisosEnviados`, `PrimerAviso`, `UltimoAviso`, `Productor`, and `ClienteID`, and SHALL treat every column of that set that is absent from the header row as an absent value for every row.
3. WHEN the header row of an uploaded file contains both `Policy number` and `Cancellation date` and does not contain `Poliza`, matched after removing leading and trailing whitespace and matched without case sensitivity, THE Cancellation_Importer SHALL classify the file as the `avisos` column set, SHALL accept the columns `Customer`, `Policy number`, `Carrier`, `Cancellation date`, `Days remaining`, `Aviso`, `Email`, `Phone`, `Asunto`, `MensajeEmail`, and `MensajeSMS`, and SHALL treat every column of that set that is absent from the header row as an absent value for every row.
4. WHEN a CSV file is uploaded, THE Cancellation_Importer SHALL propose a mapping of each file header to the column of the classified column set whose name equals that file header after removing leading and trailing whitespace and matched without case sensitivity, SHALL leave a file header with no equal column name unmapped, SHALL allow a user holding Manager_Role to change every proposed mapping before loading, and SHALL block mapping confirmation until the policy number column and the cancellation effective date column are mapped.
5. WHEN a column mapping is confirmed, THE Cancellation_Importer SHALL display an import preview containing the data row count of the file, the count of rows that will create a Cancellation_Case, the count of rows that will update an existing Cancellation_Case, the count of rows rejected by validation, and the count of duplicate rows, and SHALL create zero Cancellation_Case rows and zero Contact_Recipient rows until a user holding Manager_Role confirms the displayed import preview.
6. IF the policy number of an imported row is empty after removing leading and trailing whitespace, or the cancellation effective date value of that row is unparseable under criterion 14, or that value does not name an existing calendar date, THEN THE Cancellation_Importer SHALL reject that row, SHALL create no Cancellation_Case for that row, and SHALL report the data row number of that row, counted from 1 for the first row after the header row, together with the reason for rejection.
7. THE Cancellation_Importer SHALL store, for every accepted row, the complete original row as raw imported data, including every column that is absent from the classified column set, with each field value unchanged from the source file.
8. WHEN an import completes, THE Cancellation_Importer SHALL record exactly one import audit entry containing the file name, the classified column set, the importing profile, the confirmed column mapping, the five row counts from criterion 5, the data row number and reason of every rejected row, and the import completion time.
9. IF at least one row is rejected by validation, THEN THE Cancellation_Importer SHALL create or update the Cancellation_Case of every accepted row, SHALL create zero Cancellation_Case rows for the rejected rows, and SHALL report the data row number and the reason of every rejected row.
10. THE Cancellation_Importer SHALL parse a `MontoDebido` value that consists of an optional currency symbol, one or more digits with optional comma thousands separators, and an optional decimal point followed by one or two digits, SHALL accept a parsed value in the inclusive range 0.00 to 999,999,999.99, and SHALL store an accepted value as an amount due with two decimal places.
11. THE Cancellation_Importer SHALL store the imported `AvisosEnviados`, `PrimerAviso`, `UltimoAviso`, `Aviso`, and `Days remaining` values as legacy imported values, SHALL exclude those values from Communication_Status determination, SHALL exclude those values from Touchpoint scheduling, and SHALL exclude those values from Message_Renderer template selection.
12. THE Cancellation_Importer SHALL store an imported `Asunto`, `MensajeEmail`, or `MensajeSMS` value as a legacy reference message, and SHALL exclude those values from Message_Renderer template selection.
13. ### Requirement 9: Cancellation Case Identity and Duplicates

**User Story:** As a manager, I want repeated imports to be safe, so that reloading the same report does not create duplicate cases or duplicate messages.

#### Acceptance Criteria

WHEN an imported row matches the identity of an existing Cancellation_Case, THE Cancellation_Importer SHALL overwrite on that Cancellation_Case only the values sourced from the import file (customer name, carrier, amount due, client identifier, producer label, legacy imported values, and raw imported row), and SHALL count the row as an update.

4. WHEN a single import file contains two or more rows with the same Cancellation_Case identity, THE Cancellation_Importer SHALL load the row with the lowest source row number, SHALL count every other row carrying that identity as a duplicate, SHALL exclude every counted duplicate row from Cancellation_Case creation and from Cancellation_Case update, and SHALL record the source row number of every counted duplicate row in the import audit entry.
5. WHERE the imported client identifier of a row contains at least one non-whitespace character, THE Cancellation_Importer SHALL set the customer matching key of the Cancellation_Case to that client identifier with leading and trailing whitespace removed, with leading zeros preserved, and with no other character changed.
6. THE Cancellation_Importer SHALL map an imported producer label to an assigned employee by comparing the normalized producer label with the normalized label of every entry in the assignment mapping, and SHALL form each normalized label by removing leading and trailing whitespace, replacing every run of internal whitespace with a single space, and converting every lower-case letter to upper case.

WHEN the Cancellation_Importer updates an existing Cancellation_Case, THE Cancellation_Importer SHALL preserve the existing Case_Status, the existing Communication_Status, every Communication_Record row, every note, every evidence attachment, every recorded customer response, every audit timeline entry, every Contact_Recipient row added or edited after the previous import together with its preferred language, suppression states, and authorization status, and any employee assignment recorded by a profile holding Manager_Role.

### Requirement 10: Contact Recipient Normalization

5. THE Cancellation_Importer SHALL convert every character of an email segment to lower case and SHALL store the result as the email value of that Contact_Recipient.
6. WHEN two Contact_Recipient rows of the same Cancellation_Case and the same channel hold equal stored values after normalization, THE Cancellation_Importer SHALL retain the Contact_Recipient created from the segment appearing earlier in the imported cell and SHALL discard the later Contact_Recipient.

THE Cancellation_Importer SHALL set the validation status of each Contact_Recipient to exactly one of the values valid and invalid, SHALL derive that value only from that Contact_Recipient's own stored value, and SHALL set the validation status of an email Contact_Recipient to valid only for a stored email value that contains exactly one at sign, at least one character before the at sign, at least one character between the at sign and the first following dot, at least one dot after the at sign, at least two characters after the last dot, no whitespace character, and a total length of 6 to 254 characters.

9. THE Cancellations_Module SHALL store, for each Contact_Recipient, the channel restricted to the values phone and email, the normalized value of that channel, the contact name, the contact role, the preferred language, the preferred channel, the primary status restricted to the values primary and non-primary, the authorization status restricted to the values Authorized, Unknown, and Not Authorized of which Authorized and Unknown permit contact and Not Authorized prohibits contact, the SMS suppression flag, the email suppression flag, and the validation status, and SHALL store an absent contact name, contact role, preferred language, or preferred channel as an absent value.
10. WHEN the Notification_Scheduler selects recipients for a Touchpoint on a channel, THE Notification_Scheduler SHALL include every Contact_Recipient of the Cancellation_Case whose channel matches that channel (phone Contact_Recipient rows for the SMS channel and email Contact_Recipient rows for the email channel), whose validation status is valid, whose authorization status permits contact, and whose suppression flag for that channel is not set, and SHALL exclude every other Contact_Recipient of that Cancellation_Case.
11. IF the reduced form of a phone segment matches none of the three forms in criterion 4, THEN THE Cancellation_Importer SHALL store the whitespace-trimmed segment unchanged as the phone value, SHALL set the validation status of that Contact_Recipient to invalid, and SHALL complete the import of the Cancellation_Case.
12. IF a stored email value fails at least one of the conditions in criterion 7, THEN THE Cancellation_Importer SHALL set the validation status of that Contact_Recipient to invalid and SHALL complete the import of the Cancellation_Case.
13. WHEN the Cancellation_Importer creates a Contact_Recipient, THE Cancellation_Importer SHALL set its authorization status to Unknown, SHALL set its SMS suppression flag to not suppressed, SHALL set its email suppression flag to not suppressed, and SHALL set its primary status to primary for the first retained Contact_Recipient of that channel in imported cell order and to non-primary for every later retained Contact_Recipient of that channel.

### Requirement 12: Reminder Schedule and Idempotency

WHERE the Cancellation_Case has at least one Contact_Recipient for a channel whose validation status is valid, whose authorization status permits contact, and whose suppression for that channel is not set, WHEN the due date of a Touchpoint equals the current business date of a Notification_Scheduler run, THE Notification_Scheduler SHALL schedule that Touchpoint for that channel.

7. IF storing a Communication_Record for an Idempotency_Key is rejected by that uniqueness constraint, THEN THE Notification_Scheduler SHALL abandon that send, SHALL send zero messages for that Idempotency_Key, SHALL leave the existing Communication_Record unchanged, and SHALL count that Idempotency_Key in the skipped total of the run summary.
8. IF the due date of a Touchpoint is earlier than the current business date of a Notification_Scheduler run and no Communication_Record exists for that Touchpoint, THEN THE Notification_Scheduler SHALL send zero messages for that Touchpoint, SHALL create zero Communication_Record rows for that Touchpoint, and SHALL count that Touchpoint in the skipped total with a skip reason indicating that the due date has passed.

THE Notification_Scheduler SHALL send an automatic Touchpoint only for a Cancellation_Case whose Case_Status is Imported or Open, and SHALL exclude a Cancellation_Case whose Case_Status is Payment Reported, Verification Pending, Reinstatement Pending, Reinstated, Cancelled, Resolved, Invalid, or Duplicate, with Case_Status evaluated at the moment the send is requested.
13. THE Notification_Scheduler SHALL return a run summary containing the current business date of the run, the number of Touchpoints evaluated, the number of messages sent, the number skipped, the number failed, and the skipped count for each of these reasons: an existing Communication_Record for the Idempotency_Key, a Touchpoint due date earlier than the current business date, no eligible Contact_Recipient for the channel, an excluded Case_Status, and absent email service credentials as described in Requirement 23 criterion 3.

3. 5. 8. IF a source row cannot be stored as a raw row that satisfies criterion 2, THEN THE Cancellation_Importer SHALL reject that row, SHALL store no raw row and no Cancellation_Case for that row, SHALL report the row number and a reason indicating that the source row could not be preserved, and SHALL continue processing the remaining rows.

### Requirement 26: Deployment and Rollback

**User Story:** As a manager, I want a safe release path, so that a problem can be reversed without data loss.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Cancellations_Module SHALL introduce database changes only through new forward-only migration files whose version prefix is within the v1.10.x series, which is the next series after the current latest migration version v1.9.7, and SHALL exclude from those files any statement that drops or truncates a table, a column, or a row created by a migration at version v1.9.7 or earlier.
2. THE Cancellations_Module SHALL leave every existing migration file at version v1.9.7 or earlier unchanged, byte for byte.
3. WHEN a deployment is rolled back by restoring the previous application code while every applied v1.10.x migration remains applied, THE Cancellations_Module SHALL retain every Cancellation_Case row, every Contact_Recipient row, every Communication_Record row, and every audit timeline row, with row counts and stored field values equal to the counts and values recorded immediately before the rollback.
4. THE Cancellations_Module SHALL provide a control that sets automatic Touchpoint sending for every Cancellation_Case to enabled or disabled, SHALL default that setting to enabled, SHALL display that control only to profiles holding Manager_Role, SHALL accept a change to that setting only from a profile holding Manager_Role, and SHALL store the new setting value, the changing profile, and the change time.
5. WHILE automatic Touchpoint sending is disabled, THE Notification_Scheduler SHALL send zero automatic messages, SHALL create zero Communication_Record rows for the Touchpoints it would otherwise have sent, and SHALL report those Touchpoints in the skipped count of the run summary defined in Requirement 12 criterion 13.
6. WHILE automatic Touchpoint sending is disabled, WHEN a user selects Send Reminder Now or Retry Failed Communication in the cancellation detail drawer, THE Cancellations_Module SHALL send the message and SHALL store one Communication_Record per Contact_Recipient and channel.
7. THE Cancellations_Module SHALL be delivered on the branch `feature/renewals-cancellations` as exactly two commits, the earlier commit titled `feat(renewals): simplify renewal workspace` carrying the Phase 1 Renewals_Module changes, and the later commit titled `feat(cancellations): add reminder and tracking workflow` carrying the Phase 2 Cancellations_Module changes.
8. THE Cancellations_Module SHALL reach a deployed environment only through a deployment manually initiated by a profile holding Manager_Role, and SHALL block every deployment that lacks a manual initiation by a profile holding Manager_Role.
9. IF a deployment of the Cancellations_Module is triggered by a commit, a push, a branch merge, or any event other than a manual initiation by a profile holding Manager_Role, THEN THE Cancellations_Module SHALL block that deployment, SHALL reach zero deployed environments through that trigger, and SHALL leave every deployed environment unchanged.
